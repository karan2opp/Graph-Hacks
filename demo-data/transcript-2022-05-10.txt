Court: Has the written statement been filed?
Ld. Counsel for Defendant: My lord, the defendant was out of the country on business till last week. We seek four weeks to file the written statement.
Plaintiff's Counsel: Objection, my lord. The defendant was served in March and has been avoiding the case. He took Rs. 8 lakh from my client in January and is now delaying the matter.
Court: One last opportunity. Written statement within two weeks, subject to costs of Rs. 3,000.
Ld. Counsel for Defendant: Obliged, my lord.
