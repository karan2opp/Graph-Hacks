Court: PW1 Deepak Arora is present for cross-examination. Proceed.
Ld. Counsel for Defendant: Mr. Arora, you say you were present when the plaintiff paid money to the defendant. When was this?
PW1: It was on 14 January 2022, in the defendant's office at Saket.
Ld. Counsel for Defendant: How much was paid?
PW1: Rs. 8 lakh in cash. I saw the defendant count the notes and sign the agreement to sell.
Ld. Counsel for Defendant: Did you see the defendant sign it yourself?
PW1: Yes, he signed in front of me and handed one copy to Madam Meera.
Plaintiff's Counsel: No re-examination, my lord.
Court: Matter be listed for defendant's evidence.
